Designing for the second visit

Launch is when the doors open, not when the work ends.

Most storefronts are designed for a first visit: the arrival, the hero and the big reveal. But the customers who matter most are the ones who come back, and they see the site very differently.

They skip the introduction. They want to know what’s new, find the thing they saw last time and get to checkout without being sold to again.

What returning customers need

A clear route to what’s new. A way back to what they looked at. An account that remembers them without making a fuss about it. None of this is glamorous, but it is where retention is won or lost.

After launch, we watch how people actually use the store, test small changes and keep the ones that help. The storefront gets better because the doors are open, not in spite of it.
