Photography is the interface

On fashion and lifestyle storefronts, the campaign isn’t decoration. It is how people navigate.

People browse fashion with their eyes before they browse with a menu. They follow a look, a colour or a face from one image to the next, and the navigation is just there to catch them when they want to go back.

That changes how a storefront should be art-directed. If the photography is doing the navigating, it needs a system: consistent light, consistent framing and a clear sequence from the campaign to the product.

Shoot for the page

We plan shoots with the page in mind: which images open a collection, which ones sit beside a price and which ones only appear on a phone. It is the difference between a beautiful lookbook and a lookbook you can actually shop.
